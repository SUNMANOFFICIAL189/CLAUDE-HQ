# CAPTURE INBOX

Frictionless capture landing zone for `/capture`. Entries are appended verbatim with an
ISO-8601 timestamp the instant they're captured — never lost, never deleted. Once routed to
their home (BACKLOG / memory / Obsidian) the status marker is updated to `filed → <dest>`;
the line stays here as the audit trail (archive-never-delete).

Status markers: `status: new` (awaiting routing) · `status: filed → <dest>` · `status: kept` (lives here).
